# Chapter 2 — Gameplay decisions

## Confirmed
- Single-player prototype, browser first; multiplayer is a later ambition only.
- Start in the home-village training area. Short guided tutorial is skippable; after it, free sparring with Sensei. Sensei remains passive during the tutorial.
- Staff first; the loadout order is staff → katana → unarmed → cipher test sleeve. No deaths; either fighter can be knocked down. Press A/confirm to recover. The Cipher entry is explicitly test-only and is not a story acquisition or a source of invented abilities.
- Hold LT/RMB to aim and lock on; aim switches to an over-the-shoulder camera anchored to Ajaye, not the fighter midpoint. RT/LMB attacks with the current weapon or unarmed move. A/Space jumps, B/Q guards, X/F interacts, L3/Shift sprints, R3/Ctrl crouches; Enter or Space confirms/get up. Lock-on chooses the nearest living target in the wilderness; manual target cycling is still deferred.
- D-pad left/right (`[` / `]` or touch `SWAP`) cycles weapons. Katana has its own blade stats. Unarmed uses one attack button for `punch → kick → grab`; the grab applies a short stagger instead of a knockdown. Staff remains wired to `CONFIG` so the original spar tuning and tests stay authoritative.
- Touch follows a Call of Duty Mobile-style layout: analog movement stick bottom-left; drag the right side of the screen to look; hold AIM on the right to zoom/lock (and keep looking with another finger) while a FIRE button appears under the left thumb; all other buttons sit bottom-right.
- Support touch, keyboard/mouse, Xbox/PlayStation/third-party controllers and detected-input annotations. Keyboard/controller buttons are remappable in the Controls page; touch supports size, opacity and a left-handed layout (per-button drag positioning is still deferred).
- Levels 1–20, XP, cash, a 24-item inventory with rarity/stat rolls, equipment slots and a passive skill tree are now real local-save progression. Gear and skills feed the same combat/movement stats as level growth; there is still no shop or economy loop beyond quest/encounter rewards. Local saves are implemented; cloud persistence deferred.
- Target 30 FPS baseline on S23+; higher refresh modes are later best-effort options, not guaranteed.

## Deferred
Physical ragdoll; manual multi-target cycling; vendor/shop economy; per-button drag placement for touch; Cipher abilities and story acquisition; live online networking/multiplayer (client-side lobby/party/chat state exists but is not wired into story mode); final art and character likeness.

## Working (built, headless-tested; owner browser test passed for the pre-visual build)
- Sparring win condition: knockdowns are points, first to 3 wins, then a result screen and restart.
- Rounds: after a knockdown and recovery, a short pause returns both fighters to their marks at full health; scores persist.
- Sensei behavior: approach, 0.5s wind-up telegraph, strike, recover, with reach and accuracy tunable in `src/config.js`.
- Defense on one guard input: tap alone to block (45% move speed), hold to keep blocking, add a direction to dodge, or press just before impact for a parry. Parry negates the hit and staggers Sensei for 1.1s; after the window closes it cannot be re-armed for 0.35s, so mashing guard does not work.
- Guard pressure: three blocked hits inside 3s break the guard for 0.9s; a parry or dodge clears the pressure.
- Crouch is posture and mobility only; it no longer avoids strikes.
- Feedback: 0.22m knockback on a landed strike, body tint during wind-up and block, hit/parry/block flashes, HUD "!" warning during a wind-up, fps readout.
- Punish: a whiffed staff swing within 3.1 units invites a shortened 0.26s Sensei wind-up.
- Robustness: load-failure message if three.js or a module does not arrive, paused updates while the tab is hidden, frame-rate-independent camera lerp, WebGL context-loss notice.
- HUD: score pips, phase label (TUTORIAL / FREE SPAR / SPAR COMPLETE), knockdown health at zero until recovery.
- Analog movement: stick tilt sets travel speed (half tilt = half speed); keyboard diagonals are normalized. Camera has yaw and pitch, with drag/right-stick look.
- Aim mode: FOV eases 55→42; the camera moves from the shared orbit to a fixed over-the-shoulder view roughly 4.1m behind, 0.68m to the right and 1.55m high, looking along the player’s facing. A centre reticle appears. On touch the right STAFF button is replaced by a left FIRE button.
- Touch look area is the right 45% of the canvas; the left side belongs to the joystick. During locked aim, horizontal drag is a small camera offset around the fixed shoulder view and recentres 0.45s after the last drag; vertical drag controls aim pitch.
- All sensitivities, deadzones, FOV and camera distance are placeholder values in `src/config.js`.
- Shell: minimal title screen with only the game title and Start, Resume (disabled until a save exists), Settings and a visible but disabled “Multiplayer · in development” button. Start/Enter opens the story introduction; in play Start/Esc opens the menu; Select/Tab/M opens the map. The menu pages are CHARACTER (level/XP/cash/stats/tower state), INVENTORY (equipped slots and item cards), SKILLS (passive tree and points), QUESTS (one-active selection list), MAP (world-scaled regions/POIs), SETTINGS (look/quality + remapping + touch layout) and SYSTEM (save/delete, dev codes, multiplayer note).
- Save: SYSTEM → SAVE GAME writes tutorial completion, progression (level/XP/cash), inventory/equipment, skill points/ranks, permissions, quests and tower state to `five-sleeves.save.v1` at schema version 2. Older v1 slots with flat XP/cash migrate into the progression object and keep the starter kit. Resume restores them; Delete removes the slot. Settings, bindings and touch layout persist separately.
- Quests: five starter objectives share existing progress hooks — reach the enemy outpost, knock down all four wilderness raiders, enter the First Tower, link its five channels, or win a spar. Only one quest can be active; selecting another replaces it. Completing the active quest clears tracking and pays XP/cash once. If the objective is in another zone, the arrow points at the appropriate gate first. Older saves that used the `first-tower` ID migrate to `scout-outpost` on restore. No new canon story, faction, dialogue or reward economy was invented.
- Minimap/map: the HUD radar is north-up and player-centred: north is up, Ajaye stays fixed at the centre, and only the gold heading marker rotates with facing. Targets are projected in world axes and clamp to the rim; a cyan arrow marks the active quest. Menu maps are north-up, derive their bounds/regions/POIs from `worldmap.js`, and show the tracked quest in cyan. The larger map is ready for more regions by data alone; it is a test-world map, not approved story geography.
- Dev codes: `DEV-XP-500`, `DEV-CASH-1000`, `DEV-GEAR-SLEEVE` and `DEV-GEAR-FIELD` grant real XP through the level curve, cash, permissions and items. They remain developer shortcuts, not story rewards.
- HUD: north-up minimap top-left with a high-contrast gold player marker at the centre and an independently rotating heading arrow; health/stamina top-right; target health/phase top-centre below MENU; SKIP TUTORIAL beside MENU; weapon chip, spar score and active-quest tracker beneath the target bar; event feed under the minimap and visible in landscape. The minimap range is 24m in the yard, 52m in the wilderness and 34m in the tower. The radar is also a map shortcut on touch/desktop. Stamina drains while sprinting and is spent by jumps/dodges. The sleeve bar is hidden unless the `TEST` sleeve is equipped, in which case it is marked as test content; no capacity, charge or ability is modelled.
- The FPS readout reads `60 FPS · 277 draws · 27k tris` and is sampled over one second. Draws are renderer draw calls and tris are the approximate triangle count in that second; frame rate naturally changes while the camera/scene moves. The SYSTEM page explains the values.
- Device support: iPadOS is detected as a touch device even when Safari reports a Macintosh user agent, so touch controls remain available. A connected controller does not steal the touch-control surface; it changes the active input label/prompts only when a stick or button is actually used. Android/iOS can install the game to the home screen via the PWA manifest and service worker (HTTPS required).
- Wilderness strike robustness: a raider's strike now applies damage to the player's fighter state, not the Three.js mesh, so reaching the player can no longer throw `player.hit is not a function`. A regression test covers the hit path.
- Character articulation (2026-10-22): hips/knees and the free shoulder/elbow bend for walking, crouching and attack counterbalance. Crouch is a pose rather than a root squash; the boots stay at the knee group’s planted offsets. The staff and katana are mounted at the right hand so they follow the grip through ready/block/swing. Menu tabs no longer shrink when the menu body scrolls.
- Visual pass (2026-10-22): the arena is now a repaired settlement training yard rather than a bare ring — patched perimeter fence and gate, workshop, house with warm windows, market awning, rain barrel/hand pump, solar array and battery, air-scrubber fan, laundry, crates/barrels, staff rack, trees, distant water tower/mast and a green-grey fogline. Ajaye and Sensei are low-poly whitebox silhouettes with clothing, hair, staff and simple faces. Ajaye has no story-acquired Cipher Sleeve; the visible sleeve is a left-arm-only `TEST` mesh for the temporary loadout slot. Owner reference sheets are still needed before claiming likeness.
- Wilderness slice (2026-10-22): the north gate opens via USE/F into a wide open route (x ≈ ±36, z −112 to −13.2) with a worn track, scrub, boundary posts, rocks and dead trees. Four separate raiders (`raider-1`…`raider-4`) hold the route at z −22.5, −33, −45 and −55; two carry staffs and two katanas. Their HP scales from 45 to 54 by encounter, their names/abilities/weaknesses vary, and each stays down when defeated while dropping a prototype item and XP/cash. Approaching within `CONFIG.wildOutpostReach` (6.4m) of the enemy outpost at `(0, −63)` records outpost progress; the road continues to the First Tower at `(0, −91)`. Raiders do not award spar points and Sensei AI is disabled while outside the yard.
- Solid scenery (2026-10-22): reachable yard buildings, stalls, trees, bushes, crates, barrels, sacks, gate/ring/laundry posts, wilderness rocks/dead trees/posts, distant structures, the outpost and the First Tower's walls/pylons/console publish circle or rotated-box colliders. `collision.js` resolves the player, Sensei and raiders out of them and the existing zone bounds are applied between passes. The outpost trigger radius sits outside its solid footprint so reaching the marker still completes the objective. The tower's south doorway is a real opening, so the control chamber is walkable.
- Story introduction and title (2026-10-22): the title is only `THE 5 SLEEVES` plus Start, Resume, Install, Settings and the disabled Multiplayer button. Start/Enter opens a five-page offline story presentation (`src/story.js`) with NEXT, a final button that becomes EXIT, and a top-of-box SKIP STORY that asks for inline confirmation. Resume still bypasses the introduction; the opening pages are broad mood/setting text and do not add canon, named geography, Cipher powers or tower activation. A separate reusable tower deck presents arrival, the five channels and a silent local link after the player uses the console.
- HUD/loadout: a weapon chip shows the current loadout; the target panel shows Sensei or the nearest living raider; spar score pips hide in the wilderness. Radar/map nodes show living raiders. The sleeve vital appears only while the test sleeve is equipped and is marked as test content.
- Progression and gear (2026-10-22): levels 1–20 follow an `80 × 1.22^(level−1)` XP curve; each level grants one skill point and small base HP/stamina/attack/defence growth. Cash, quest/encounter rewards and dev codes feed the same ledger. The starter kit (training staff, patched vest, field wrap) gives real bonuses; a 24-slot inventory holds prototype common-to-legendary weapons, armour and gear with value, level requirement and stat rolls. Weapon bonuses only count while that weapon kind is active; armour/gear always count. The passive skill tree has movement, defence, conditioning and striking nodes, all simple stat effects. This is prototype progression, not canon power growth.
- Enemy vision and defence (2026-10-22): wilderness raiders have a vision cone (22m by default), a calm → suspicious (`?`) → spotted (`!`) alert cycle with a short memory, and auto-approach once spotted. In melee they can block, parry and dodge using the same nonlethal rules as the player, but their HP, reach and damage stay below the player's. Only enemies show `?`/`!`; the player never gets an exclamation marker. Blocking three times without a parry/dodge can break a raider's guard.
- First Tower (2026-10-22): the wilderness now continues past the outpost to a large walkable tower at `CONFIG.towerZ = -91`. It has an open south doorway, an outer monumental wall, an inner control chamber, a central dais, five quiet channel pylons and a south-side console. Entering sets tower state; using the console sets `towerLinked`, awards XP/cash and a guardian plate, and opens a short tower story deck. The effect is deliberately a local prototype: the channels answer, but Cipher does not speak and no new power, module rule or canon activation effect is defined. Exact activation behaviour and first Cipher speech remain owner-approval items.
- Cipher tactical scan (2026-10-22): while aiming with the test-only Cipher loadout, the HUD shows a limited Iron-Man-style readout for the locked target: display name, level, HP/health bar, alert state, known abilities, observed weaknesses and a LIVE/CACHED confidence tag. It does not expose intent, memory, lore or unapproved Sleeve powers. The readout is a test of presentation and targeting, not a story acquisition.
- Multiplayer-ready client scaffolding (2026-10-22): `src/multiplayer.js` is a pure, transport-agnostic lobby/presence/party/chat state machine with bounded text, readiness, party-size limits, a serialisable snapshot and a drained outbox for a future adapter. It is intentionally not imported by `game.js`, `main.js` or the story shell; the title/System multiplayer entries remain disabled placeholders. Live networking needs a backend/transport that does not exist yet.
- Pause/settings: menu freezes simulation; look sensitivity (0.4–2×), invert-Y and graphics quality (performance 1×, balanced ≤1.5×, sharp ≤2.5× pixel ratio) plus remapping and touch layout all persist in local storage.
- Browser status: the owner tested the 2026-10-22 settlement/character visual pass on the S23+ and reported successful play with no frame drops. The progression/inventory/skills, tower, scan, enemy-vision and north-up minimap passes are headless-tested only here; they still need an owner browser/device pass for layout, readability, camera framing, scan overlay, tower interior and performance. Touch layout remains intentionally deferred.
