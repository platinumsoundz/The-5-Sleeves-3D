# The 5 Sleeves — Training Ground & Wilderness Slice

This is the procedural training-yard and wilderness prototype. Serve it over HTTP and open `localhost:8000`. It requires WebGL and three.js from the CDN; the in-game world is procedural, and the only raster art is the PWA icon set. It is now an installable PWA: `manifest.webmanifest` plus `sw.js` cache the app shell and the pinned three.js module, so an installed copy can start offline after one successful online visit. The five runtime PWA icons under `icons/` are generated from the owner-supplied source at `icons/source/app-icon.png`.

The playable loop is still nonlethal: train with Sensei, then spar to the first three knockdowns. The north gate opens into a wide wilderness route with four raiders, an occupied enemy outpost, and a walkable First Tower beyond it. D-pad left/right (or `[` / `]` on keyboard, `SWAP` on touch) cycles `staff → katana → unarmed → cipher test sleeve`; the staff remains first so the existing tutorial and spar flow are unchanged. Unarmed uses one attack button for a `punch → kick → grab` sequence; the grab staggers rather than scoring a knockdown. Raiders use separate encounters with vision cones, `?`/`!` alert markers, auto-approach and player-like block/parry/dodge reactions; they do not award spar points and stay down when defeated. No deaths, save/menu behavior and first-to-three sparring are preserved. Players, Sensei and raiders resolve against solid scenery instead of walking through buildings, crates, trees, the outpost or the tower walls. Levels, XP, cash, a 24-slot inventory, equipment bonuses and a passive skill tree are now real local-save progression.

**Test-only content:** the `CIPHER SLEEVE · TEST` loadout exists only to exercise an equipment slot, a left-arm mesh and a limited aiming scan. It is not a story acquisition, does not unlock or invent Cipher powers, and is labelled as developer test content in the HUD and gear page. Ajaye still has no story-acquired Sleeve in this opening scene. The scan shows only observed tactical data — level, vitals, known abilities, physical weaknesses and a LIVE/CACHED tag — never intent, memory, future powers or canon lore. The test mesh is left-arm-only, as required if a Sleeve is ever shown.

Keyboard/mouse: WASD/arrows move, left-click attack, `[`/`]` cycle weapons, right-click hold to aim/lock, Space jump/get up, Shift sprint, Ctrl crouch, Q (or K) guard — tap to block, direction to dodge, time it just before impact to parry — F interact/use, Enter confirm; drag to orbit and pitch the camera. Standard gamepad: left stick move, right stick look, D-pad left/right cycle weapons, LT aim/lock, RT attack, A jump/get up, B guard, X interact/confirm, L3 sprint, R3 crouch. Touch uses the existing mobile-shooter layout: analog stick bottom-left, drag the right side to look, hold AIM for the fixed over-the-shoulder view, `SWAP` cycles weapons, and the other buttons sit bottom-right. Touch-button size and placement remain deferred at the owner's request.

At the north gate, stand near it and press USE/F to move into the wilderness; USE/F at the gate on the wilderness side returns to the yard. Follow the track to the enemy outpost, then continue to the First Tower. Its south door is open; walk inside to the control chamber. USE/F at the south-side console opens the local link and shows a short tower scene. The channels answer, but Cipher does not speak and no activation power or wider-world effect is defined yet. The raiders use the same nonlethal knockdown/recovery rules as the spar but are separate from Sensei and the match score. The camera follows Ajaye in the wilderness/tower and the over-the-shoulder aim stays player-anchored.

Start or Enter on the title opens a five-page offline story introduction. NEXT advances; the final page's button becomes EXIT. SKIP STORY sits at the top of the story box and asks for confirmation before going straight to the yard. The pages are data-driven in `src/story.js` so later story events can reuse the deck without changing the overlay. The title screen itself is reduced to the game title and buttons.

MENU → QUESTS holds the current quest log. Five starter quests cover reaching the enemy outpost, clearing the four raiders, entering the First Tower, linking its five channels, and winning a spar; only one quest can be active at a time. The active quest is shown in a small HUD tracker, marked in cyan on both menu maps, and tracked by an arrow on the minimap. Quest progress and the active selection are saved. The minimap is north-up and bird's-eye: north is up, Ajaye stays at its centre, and only the gold heading marker rotates with facing, so the map never spins under the player. Targets use world axes and clamp to the rim; range is 24m in the yard, 52m in the wilderness and 34m in the tower. The menu maps are projected from data-driven world bounds and regions in `src/worldmap.js`, so adding another open-world region only needs another entry in that catalogue. The tower and outpost are test-world locations, not approved final story geography.

The tutorial guides movement, attack, aim/lock and guard/dodge/parry and can be skipped. Sensei is passive until spar, then telegraphs with a HUD `!` before striking. Both fighters can be knocked down and recover; a downed player must confirm to get up. Three blocked hits in a row break the guard. The HUD puts the minimap top-left, player health/stamina top-right, and the current target's health/phase top-centre below MENU; SKIP TUTORIAL sits beside MENU and the event feed sits under the minimap, including in landscape. MENU or Esc pauses and opens CHARACTER, INVENTORY, SKILLS, QUESTS, MAP, SETTINGS and SYSTEM pages. Levels 1–20 grant one skill point each; the passive tree, starter gear and weapon loadout all feed real attack, defence, speed and vitality. Cash and item values are prototype economy only. The test sleeve's scan is a limited tactical readout, not a Cipher ability.

The FPS readout is now labelled `60 FPS · 277 draws · 27k tris`: frames per second, renderer draw calls and roughly the number of triangles rendered in the last second. FPS changing while moving is expected; the performance note in SYSTEM explains the values.

The Cipher Sleeve HUD meter stays hidden unless the test sleeve is equipped. Hips, knees and the free arm are articulated for walking, crouching and attack counterbalance; boots stay planted and the staff/katana is mounted in the right hand. Character likeness still requires approved reference sheets. The 2026-10-22 settlement/character visual pass was device-tested by the owner without reported frame drops; **the progression/inventory/skills, enemy-vision/defence, First Tower, scan, north-up minimap and tower-story pass has not been browser- or device-rendered here**, so its menu layout, gear/skill cards, tower interior, camera framing, scan overlay, minimap feel, raider silhouettes, collision feel and performance still need an owner pass. Run `npm test` for the 89 headless checks; the in-game FPS readout also reports draw calls and triangles for device checks. See `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/memory/02-gameplay.md`, provenance in `docs/memory/04-ai-collaboration.md`, and `docs/ai-build-log.md`.

**Multiplayer:** `src/multiplayer.js` now contains pure, transport-agnostic lobby/presence/party/chat state, bounded text, readiness and an outbox for a future adapter. It is deliberately **not imported by story mode**; the title and System multiplayer entries remain disabled placeholders. Live networking needs a backend/transport and an owner-approved mode boundary. The tower's exact activation effect and whether Cipher speaks on first link also remain owner-approval items; the current scene keeps Cipher silent.

**Install:** Android/Chromium may show an INSTALL APP button on the title screen; otherwise use the browser menu → Install app. On iPhone/iPad use Share → Add to Home Screen. Installation needs HTTPS (or localhost) and the site must be served from a real origin rather than opening the HTML directly.

**Icon artwork:** the owner-supplied source at `icons/source/app-icon.png` has been exported to the runtime files `icons/icon-192.png`, `icons/icon-512.png`, `icons/icon-maskable-512.png`, `icons/apple-touch-icon.png` and `icons/favicon-32.png`. You do not need to replace those five by hand. If the source artwork changes, replace only `icons/source/app-icon.png`, regenerate all five exports and bump the `CACHE` version in `sw.js`; keeping the filenames stable preserves the manifest and iOS meta tag. Details are in `icons/README.md`.

Current preview is commit-pinned because raw.githack's `/main/` path can serve cached modules: `https://raw.githack.com/platinumsoundz/The-5-Sleeves-3D/0fcbb06/index.html`.
