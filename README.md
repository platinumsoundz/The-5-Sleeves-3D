# The 5 Sleeves — Training Ground & Wilderness Slice

This is the procedural training-yard and wilderness prototype. Serve it over HTTP and open `localhost:8000`. It requires WebGL and three.js from the CDN; no external art assets are needed. It is now an installable PWA: `manifest.webmanifest` plus `sw.js` cache the app shell and the pinned three.js module, so an installed copy can start offline after one successful online visit. The five runtime PWA icons under `icons/` are generated from the owner-supplied source at `icons/source/app-icon.png`.

The playable loop is still nonlethal: train with Sensei, then spar to the first three knockdowns. The north gate opens into a wide wilderness route with four raiders and an occupied enemy outpost at the end of the test slice. D-pad left/right (or `[` / `]` on keyboard, `SWAP` on touch) cycles `staff → katana → unarmed → cipher test sleeve`; the staff remains first so the existing tutorial and spar flow are unchanged. Unarmed uses one attack button for a `punch → kick → grab` sequence; the grab staggers rather than scoring a knockdown. Raiders use separate encounters and do not award spar points. No deaths, save/menu behavior and first-to-three sparring are preserved. Players, Sensei and raiders now resolve against solid scenery instead of walking through buildings, crates, trees and the outpost.

**Test-only content:** the `CIPHER SLEEVE · TEST` loadout exists only to exercise an equipment slot and a left-arm mesh. It is not a story acquisition, does not unlock or invent Cipher powers, and is labelled as developer test content in the HUD and gear page. Ajaye still has no story-acquired Sleeve in this opening scene. The test mesh is left-arm-only, as required if a Sleeve is ever shown.

Keyboard/mouse: WASD/arrows move, left-click attack, `[`/`]` cycle weapons, right-click hold to aim/lock, Space jump/get up, Shift sprint, Ctrl crouch, Q (or K) guard — tap to block, direction to dodge, time it just before impact to parry — F interact/use, Enter confirm; drag to orbit and pitch the camera. Standard gamepad: left stick move, right stick look, D-pad left/right cycle weapons, LT aim/lock, RT attack, A jump/get up, B guard, X interact/confirm, L3 sprint, R3 crouch. Touch uses the existing mobile-shooter layout: analog stick bottom-left, drag the right side to look, hold AIM for the fixed over-the-shoulder view, `SWAP` cycles weapons, and the other buttons sit bottom-right. Touch-button size and placement remain deferred at the owner's request.

At the north gate, stand near it and press USE/F to move into the wilderness; USE/F at the gate on the wilderness side returns to the yard. Follow the track to the enemy outpost. Reaching its watch radius ends the current test slice. The raiders use the same nonlethal knockdown/recovery rules as the spar but are separate from Sensei and the match score. The camera follows Ajaye in the wilderness and the over-the-shoulder aim stays player-anchored.

Start or Enter on the title opens a five-page offline story introduction. NEXT advances; the final page's button becomes EXIT. SKIP STORY sits at the top of the story box and asks for confirmation before going straight to the yard. The pages are data-driven in `src/story.js` so later story events can reuse the deck without changing the overlay. The title screen itself is reduced to the game title and buttons.

MENU → QUESTS holds the current quest log. Three starter quests cover reaching the enemy outpost, clearing the four raiders and winning a spar; only one quest can be active at a time. The active quest is shown in a small HUD tracker, marked in cyan on both menu maps, and tracked by an arrow on the player-centred minimap. Quest progress and the active selection are saved. The minimap is a follow view: Ajaye stays at its centre with a high-contrast gold marker, while nearby Sensei/raider and quest targets move around the ring. Its follow range is 24m in the yard and 52m in the wilderness, so it covers a larger area. The menu maps are projected from data-driven world bounds and regions in `src/worldmap.js`, so adding another open-world region only needs another entry in that catalogue.

The tutorial guides movement, attack, aim/lock and guard/dodge/parry and can be skipped. Sensei is passive until spar, then telegraphs with a HUD `!` before striking. Both fighters can be knocked down and recover; a downed player must confirm to get up. Three blocked hits in a row break the guard. The HUD now puts the minimap top-left, player health/stamina top-right, and Sensei's 1v1 boss health top-centre below MENU; SKIP TUTORIAL sits beside MENU and the event feed sits under the minimap, including in landscape. MENU or Esc pauses and opens look sensitivity, invert-Y and graphics-quality settings, stored locally. XP/currency and Cipher powers remain deferred.

The FPS readout is now labelled `60 FPS · 277 draws · 27k tris`: frames per second, renderer draw calls and roughly the number of triangles rendered in the last second. FPS changing while moving is expected; the performance note in SYSTEM explains the values.

The Cipher Sleeve HUD meter stays hidden unless the test sleeve is equipped. Hips, knees and the free arm are articulated for walking, crouching and attack counterbalance; boots stay planted and the staff/katana is mounted in the right hand. Character likeness still requires approved reference sheets. The 2026-10-22 settlement/character visual pass was device-tested by the owner without reported frame drops; **the weapon/wilderness/raider/quest/HUD/PWA and collision/story/title pass has not been browser- or device-rendered here**, so its camera framing, raider silhouettes, sleeve test mesh, minimap arrow, menu quest cards, HUD spacing, collision feel, story overlay, title layout and install prompt still need an owner pass. Run `npm test` for the 72 headless checks; the in-game FPS readout also reports draw calls and triangles for device checks. See `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/memory/02-gameplay.md`, provenance in `docs/memory/04-ai-collaboration.md`, and `docs/ai-build-log.md`.

**Install:** Android/Chromium may show an INSTALL APP button on the title screen; otherwise use the browser menu → Install app. On iPhone/iPad use Share → Add to Home Screen. Installation needs HTTPS (or localhost) and the site must be served from a real origin rather than opening the HTML directly.

**Icon artwork:** the owner-supplied source at `icons/source/app-icon.png` has been exported to the runtime files `icons/icon-192.png`, `icons/icon-512.png`, `icons/icon-maskable-512.png`, `icons/apple-touch-icon.png` and `icons/favicon-32.png`. You do not need to replace those five by hand. If the source artwork changes, replace only `icons/source/app-icon.png` and regenerate all five exports; keeping the filenames stable preserves the manifest, iOS meta tag and service-worker cache. Details are in `icons/README.md`.
