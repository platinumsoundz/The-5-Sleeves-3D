# The 5 Sleeves — Training Ground

Current visual-pass preview (commit-pinned because raw.githack's `/main/` path can serve a cached module): https://raw.githack.com/platinumsoundz/The-5-Sleeves-3D/4b1b564/index.html

Serve over HTTP and open localhost:8000. Requires WebGL and Three.js CDN. This is a procedural settlement training scene with low-poly character whiteboxes; no external art assets are required.

Keyboard/mouse (standard shooter layout): WASD/arrows move, left-click staff attack (click, not drag), right-click hold to aim/lock, Space jump/get up, Shift sprint, Ctrl crouch, Q (or K) guard — tap to block, direction to dodge, time it just before impact to parry — F interact, Enter confirm; drag to orbit and pitch the camera. Standard gamepad: left stick move, right stick look, LT aim/lock, RT staff attack, A jump/get up, B guard (direction dodges, timed parry), X interact/confirm, L3 sprint, R3 crouch. Touch uses a mobile-shooter layout: analog stick bottom-left, drag the right side to look, hold AIM to switch to a fixed over-the-shoulder view and a FIRE button appears over the left thumb; the other buttons sit bottom-right. Tutorial guides movement, staff attack, aim/lock, and guard/dodge/parry and can be skipped. Sensei is passive until spar, then telegraphs with a HUD "!" before striking. Both fighters can be knocked down and recover; a downed player must confirm to get up. Knockdowns score and the first to 3 takes the spar; three blocked hits in a row break the guard. Restart practice appears after tutorial. MENU (top-right) or Esc pauses and opens look sensitivity, invert-Y and graphics-quality settings, stored locally. The Cipher Sleeve is not acquired in this opening scene, so its HUD meter is hidden and Ajaye has only wrapped forearms. Hips, knees and the free arm are articulated for walking, crouching and attack counterbalance; boots stay planted and the staff is mounted in the right hand. Weaponless combat is a scoped proposal only, not implemented yet. XP/currency and Cipher powers are deferred. The owner reports successful device/browser tests with no frame drops after the 2026-10-22 visual pass; check the on-screen FPS/draw-call/triangle readout after future content changes. Run `npm test` for the 53 headless checks; the in-game FPS readout also reports draw calls and triangles for device checks.
