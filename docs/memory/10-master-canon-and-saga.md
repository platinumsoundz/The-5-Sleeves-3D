## Premise, themes, and timeline

Near-future science-fiction/fantasy, circa 2036, approximately five years after a modern collapse and ten years after commercial Sleeve launch. Everyday life should remain recognisably contemporary: homes, families, food, patched phones/laptops, markets, farms, workshops, familiar transport, jokes and arguments. The extraordinary technology is concentrated in ancient towers, spirit-linked Sleeve systems, and remnants of an advanced civilisation. Avoid generic cyberpunk, ubiquitous holograms, effortless flying vehicles, and a uniformly ruined/unbreathable world.

Themes: capability does not erase human limits; dependence on systems without maintenance knowledge creates vulnerability; power requires practice and responsibility; spirits are agents; restoring a system is not automatically making the world better; control can reproduce prior failures; Ajaye's ingenuity is shown by observing, testing, repairing, learning, and adapting rather than instant answers.

Timeline anchors: commercial Sleeves launched in 2026 (provisional date anchor); modern catastrophe around 2031; Ajaye's story around 2036. An ancient catastrophe happened at an unknown time. Towers became important after it and relate to environmental stability/recovery. The ancient catastrophe and modern collapse are distinct. Exact causal links between either catastrophe, tower failures, the virus/Darkness, AI/cloud failures, infrastructure and social collapse remain open. Do not conflate causes or claim a sequence without approved evidence.

## World, environment, and survival

Infrastructure, communications, manufacturing, education, archives and supply chains have degraded; humanity is not biologically less intelligent. Digital records can survive but be inaccessible due to power, hardware, interfaces, permissions, credentials, software, specialist tools, or missing knowledge. Cloud failure alone does not explain total knowledge loss.

Some heavily populated or damaged regions require clean-air infrastructure and filters; outside air is not uniformly unsafe. Risk varies with region and weather. Fog and environmental effects require local depiction, protection, route warnings and maintenance where relevant. Crops, water, livestock and wildlife may show uneven recent changes, but their permanence and cause are unknown. Earlier crisis-related mutations mostly faded or diminished while towers stabilised the planet; current instability may be allowing unusual signs to return, but biology remains open.

Significant resources need credible sources: carried stock, farming/fishing, rationing, scavenging, trade, a shown local source, established cache, workshop, or specific ally. Power/charging, filters, medicine and protective gear require a functioning source and condition. No routine deliveries across wastelands or plot-critical item appearing from nowhere. Travel can use walking, bicycles, rentable scooters/hover-assist on suitable routes, utility vehicles, boats/ferries, cargo lifts and repaired transit. Battery range, terrain, weather, charging, tolls, maintenance and spares matter. Show meaningful journey costs and consequences without accounting for every trivial item.

## Sleeves, energy, hosts, spirits, and Cipher

Commercial Sleeves launched in 2026 after secret Apple/Samsung development, became popular, and remain in use despite scarce repairs. Their exact relationship to ancient ability-bearing Sleeves is **Open**; never assume identical systems.

Ajaye discovers an ancient beta Cipher Sleeve, which bonds to his arm. It can be gradually customised, but this is a **Proposed Canon** direction with bounded requirements: parts, tools, time, data, physical access, testing, compatible hardware/software and permissions. Modifications can fail, overheat, misread data or cause side effects. Improvements can expand visibility, efficiency, interfaces and access; they cannot grant unlimited energy or bypass every restriction. Ajaye's green Sleeve is on one arm only, exact side and construction as approved sheet; in this project, owner-confirmed left arm. Do not mirror/duplicate it.

Three factors are distinct but interacting: (1) finite internal Sleeve energy; (2) host physical/mental condition (focus, endurance, coordination, fatigue, emotional regulation); (3) spirit agency and willingness. Charging does not cure fatigue; rest does not recharge hardware. Skill increases efficiency/control, not limitless output. Exact battery chemistry, capacity, charging, costs and numerical thresholds are open—avoid invented percentages/units.

Cipher begins dormant/partial with incomplete memory/access. Tasks can require compatible apps/plugins, permission/credentials, physical interface, functioning data, sufficient energy and host capacity. Data may be corrupt, encrypted, incomplete or misleading. Capability is not access. Cipher is the only spirit currently established as able to build the antivirus, but cannot do so initially; access, data, software, credentials and restored network must be earned.

Current proposed story rule: Cipher first genuinely communicates with Ajaye upon first tower activation. Earlier signs are nonverbal anomalous readings, signals, pulses or interface reactions, not explanatory conversation. Exact first words and whether the activation immediately produces speech remain approval-sensitive (see chapter 09).

The Bible's approved-direction rule for unconsciousness: Cipher does not automatically become unconscious when Ajaye does; it enters Restricted Mode, prioritising the Spirit Link/essential monitoring, pausing or throttling intensive scans/development. During sleep it may maintain low-demand functions/link if energy and connection permit. Temporary emergency autonomy is possible only in genuine life danger, costs resources and remains access-limited. Exact thresholds and safeguards are open. Do not implement these as established game mechanics without product decisions.

Spirits are intelligent agents with identities, priorities and choices; can refuse, have incomplete information, and are not buttons, omniscient guides or automatic moral judges. Separate spirit, module, host, Sleeve, mastery, and relationship. Module possession does not equal mastery or being chosen. Spirit relationship can persist if a module is removed.

The four elemental domains are Wind, Water, Earth, Fire; Cipher is a fifth distinct identity, not an elemental domain. The original AI created the four elemental spirits; Cipher is a clone/derivative held in a beta Sleeve, containing an incomplete essence/portion of that AI. Chronology, creators, purpose and relation to ancient catastrophe stay open. Korb's Wind choice is enabled through Cipher's link; his protective action reveals character rather than manufacturing power. Wind is initially uncontrolled and stamina-costly; training improves efficiency, not all costs. Exact Korb-awakening staging has discrepancies logged in chapter 09.

## Modules, Darkness, virus, and towers

The established model references 25 original modules, with five specialised modules per domain/system. Four named elemental domains account for 20; allocation of remaining five, especially any Cipher relationship, is unresolved in the source itself. Do not invent names/functions, costs, range, roster, allocation, storage capacity, simultaneous use, or module/consent rules. Owner-approved transfer principle: modules can be taken, recovered, transferred or shared; Ajaye may eventually collect/switch among all 25, but must earn sources and cannot be presumed to use all at once. Distinguish spirit vs module vs host vs Sleeve vs mastery.

Kael family's secret extension programme aimed for 30 total and deliberately created Darkness as ability 26. The attempt corrupted the system and is connected to the virus; four later extensions were not completed. The sixth Dark spirit, 26th ability, virus, and Cipher are related mysteries but not interchangeable. A possible Cipher/Dark counterpart or completion relationship is an uncertain endgame possibility, not established fact or simple absorption/cure. Full extension design, surviving plans and Kael's knowledge remain open.

Towers are part of an ancient network needed after the ancient catastrophe and tied to environmental stability/recovery. They are not arbitrary boss arenas. A tower scene should connect journey, system issue, spirit relationship, access limits, information and consequences. Restoration can reconnect a local system, improve local conditions and reveal information; it is not a magic reset. Before developing a tower, define what characters know, required access, Cipher's current capability, immediate local effect, new information, and costs. Exact ancient engineering and local effects remain open.

The proposed endgame Rebind requires five true Sleeves and freely willing spirit cooperation; Cipher cannot command or absorb others. The restored towers/archive are needed to assemble access. This is story direction, not a finished technical specification. Consent and choice are central.

## Character direction (text remains subordinate to approved visual sheets)

- **Ajaye:** around 16 in outline; loyal, practical, curious, technically skilled and weapon-trained. Learns by demonstration/testing; rushes and treats people as puzzle pieces. Arc toward asking/listening/shared responsibility. No starting Jax; any later animal requires introduction and credible care/resources.
- **Sensei/father and Mother:** loving, distinct parents; Sensei teaches restraint/judgement and may recognise a symbol without knowing whole history. Mother practical, perceptive, protects ordinary warmth. Neither is an exposition machine.
- **Korb:** agile, expressive, funny; humour masks fear/loneliness. Emerald Wind design as approved reference. Do not make him inexplicably younger/shorter than Ajaye. His stamina limits are real.
- **Ella:** capable, independent, protective; gradual attraction through training, disagreement, kindness, honesty and some jealousy—not sudden romance or reduction to love interest.
- **Lex:** Ajaye's sister and Wayfinder; sibling competition, insults, protectiveness. Her choices account for those relying on her; her arc need not erase Wayfinders/independence.
- **Kael:** refined, severe, controlling; obsidian artificial Sleeve with red-black seams per approved reference. Inherited myths/fragments, not complete knowledge. Corruption exploits fear but does not absolve harm.
- **Oscar:** disciplined, dutiful; personal reasons to help, not a delivered power-up.
- **Reegan:** anger grounded in real harm to community; trust gradual. Outline associates him with Fire in Book Four; verify against approved story/references before use.

## Five-book outline (working, not canon by inclusion)

Book One **The Perimeter and the First Tower**: discovery, departure, Cipher, Korb/Wind, resource-constrained travel, first local tower restoration. Detailed beats in chapter 09.

Book Two **The Sunken Coast**: coastal travel, water-based settlements and transport, Ella/Tide, Lex/Wayfinders, second tower and water/weather stability. Local recovery and relationships deepen; Kael steals a fragment. Ella joins gradually; attraction remains understated.

Book Three **Shadows of Exile**: desert journey, delayed relay from home, Oscar/Earth and a settlement's practical needs, third tower/archive, revelations about Sensei's Iron-Oak past and ancient history, Kael's Sleeve damage. These inherited specifics require verification against approved manuscript; do not treat outline alone as confirmed.

Book Four **The Frozen Wastes**: cold and resource limits, Reegan/Fire and his community, forbidden vault, 26th Darkness/virus history, Kael's corruption and fourth tower. The group restores local heat regulation; Cipher access remains incomplete. All details remain outline proposals until verified.

Book Five **The Apex Citadel**: allies contribute constrained resources, Lex coordinates evacuation/routes, Ajaye obtains access via recovered credentials/adapter, Kael faces accountability, virus attacks connected systems, proposed Rebind demands voluntary cooperation, the Dark spirit's relationship with Cipher remains uncertain, and ending stabilises safeguards/reopens routes without resetting the world to perfection. Do not present antivirus, Rebind, global outcomes or final pairing as game/approved canon absent owner approval.

## Recurring scene and adaptation checks

Show what characters need, can afford, and leave behind before departure. Include meaningful travel (route, weather, fatigue, transport, stop, breakdown, person). Markets/workshops reveal people and local rules, not just item acquisition. Give major characters ordinary non-power scenes. Affection appears in practical gestures; jealousy remains human. Comedy is voice-specific. After action show pain, damage, costs, fear, gratitude, disagreement or reputation changes. Ajaye's competence coexists with blind spots and apologies; do not make him instantly right.

For game use, these rules are constraints on later story adaptation, not new scope. Inspect actual code/tests before claiming implementation; distinguish placeholders from behavior. Ask owner before choices that would settle canon, energy arithmetic, module mechanics, tower effects, or story inclusion. Keep comic requirements (portrait dimensions, panels, Canva) separate from browser/Three.js rendering.
