# Chapter 1 — Canon guardrails

**Sources received:** Consolidated Master World & Canon Bible v2.0; Revised Complete Story Outline v2.1; Book One Script Reference v1.0. Bible is the intended primary reference; outline is a working plan; script dialogue is draft. If sources conflict, flag it rather than silently resolving it.

- Setting circa 2036, near-future and recognizably contemporary; infrastructure and knowledge are unreliable.
- Ajaye is a practical fixer, technically gifted and trained in weapons. He can rush and treat people as tactical pieces; he grows by listening and sharing responsibility.
- Owner's direct game decision: Ajaye's green Cipher Sleeve is on his left arm only. Approved visual references later govern exact construction.
- Cipher is incomplete and limited. Do not invent a prototype power, module name, module roster, energy units, or early explanatory dialogue. Current story direction says Cipher first genuinely communicates after the first tower activation.
- Sleeve energy, host condition, and spirit agency are separate but interacting limits. Power has costs. Spirits are agents, not buttons.
- Tower restoration produces local improvement, not instant planetary recovery.
- Current opening is training in the village. This whitebox is a mechanics prototype, not a claim that all story beats are implemented or approved.
