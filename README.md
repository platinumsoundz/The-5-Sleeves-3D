# The 5 Sleeves — Training Ground

Serve over HTTP and open localhost:8000. Requires WebGL and Three.js CDN. This is a procedural village mechanics whitebox.

Keyboard: WASD/arrows move, J staff attack, Space jump, K dodge, Shift sprint, Ctrl crouch, E aim/lock, Enter confirm/get up. Standard gamepad: left stick move, LT aim/lock, RT staff attack, A jump, B dodge, X interact/confirm, L3 sprint, R3 crouch, Start confirm. Touch controls provide movement and actions; drag the scene to orbit the camera. Tutorial guides movement, staff attack, lock-on, and dodge/jump and can be skipped. Sensei is passive until spar. Both fighters can be knocked down and recover; a downed player must confirm to get up. Restart practice appears after tutorial. XP/currency and Cipher powers are deferred. Browser testing has not been performed. See docs.
