# Chapter 6 — Character appearance and continuity

## Visual authority and status
Approved character sheets control exact faces, proportions, clothing, accessories, colours and distinctive construction. The text below records useful descriptors from the supplied visual guide/prompt pack; it does not replace the missing reference-sheet images. Prompt-pack specs are working visual instructions; verify against approved sheets before making final models/art. Preserve one-arm placement exactly; never mirror a design to fit composition.

## Ajaye
- Book One: age 16; prompt pack gives approx. 5'6" / 168 cm, compact athletic build.
- Dense, very curly brown hair with natural spring/volume and warm chestnut highlights; blue eyes. Keep facial proportions/identity from the approved model sheet.
- Character acting: practical, alert, curious, determined, stubborn at times; capable of observation, caution, dry humour and vulnerability. Not permanently brooding/emotionless.
- Dark layered scavenger clothing: charcoal/black hood or scarf/neck layer, distressed short-sleeved outer layer, dark underlayers, worn trousers, gloves/wrist wraps, utility belt/pouches, sturdy boots. Weathered crossed/layered straps and buckles as in sheet; not pristine tactical gear or fashionable streetwear. Backpack when scene requires, consistent shape and strap placement. Do not add decorative armour/accessories without story source.
- Sleeve: owner's explicit decision + canon = green Cipher Sleeve on Ajaye's **left arm only**. Right arm remains normal human tissue. Fitted, segmented dark mechanical device, restrained cyan/blue illuminated channels, technological rather than magical; not a full robotic arm unless story explicitly says so. Keep model-sheet silhouette/channel pattern and compact integrated module slot. Confirm left from Ajaye's perspective; front view means his left appears viewer's right. Do not move it through panels/turnarounds.
- Book One prompt pack says Cipher Sleeve is absent until Ajaye discovers/bonds at Page 8; afterward one left arm only.
- Visual-guide heritage descriptor: Caucasian with one-quarter Caribbean heritage; father is described there as half-mixed Caribbean, mother as British Caucasian. This is a visual guide description, not a license to stereotype or override approved face references.

## Korb
- Prompt pack: age 17, approx. 5'9" / 175 cm, lean/athletic; dark hair under characteristic beanie; practical black/charcoal clothing with green accents, backpack/straps/boots; expressive face, quick/light body language, playful eyebrows or sideways grin.
- Emerald/green Wind Sleeve on one arm only exactly as approved sheet; distinguish from Ajaye's Cipher Sleeve. Do not show him before Book One Page 31 under current page map.
- Humor often masks fear; visual acting must also show when the joke stops.

## Sensei / Father
- Prompt pack: adult around 44, approx. 5'11" / 180 cm; long dark hair tied back, facial hair, rugged layered practical clothing, steady/protective presence. Avoid generic elderly martial-arts-master stereotype.
- He teaches restraint/judgement through stance, questions, demonstration and action; not an exposition machine. Training opens the story.

## Mother
- Prompt pack: adult around 43, approx. 5'5" / 165 cm; long dark hair, warm/observant expression; layered earth-tone clothing with green/rust accents.
- The style guide describes her as Caucasian British with green eyes; treat exact appearance as reference-sheet governed because the canon bible does not establish this ethnicity detail. Warm, practical, perceptive, protective without smothering.

## Kael

- Artificial Sleeve: dark obsidian, manufactured hard styling with red/crimson seams/output. It is not a natural true Sleeve and is not Ajaye's green Cipher or Korb's green Wind device. Do not show before Book One pages 51–55 in the prompt-pack sequence.

## Cipher (see detailed Chapter 7)

## Gear continuity

When a sword/long weapon is carried behind a backpack, preserve depth, side and continuity across shots; do not teleport it to foreground or opposite side. Track hand occupancy, pack, straps, repairs, damage, props and carried gear from panel to panel.
