# Chapter 7 — Cipher's visual design

## Status and source
**Source:** `The_5_Sleeves_Cipher_Visual_Reference_v2_0.txt`, whose header says the design direction was approved by the owner in conversation on 16 October 2026. It calls the approved Cipher character sheet the primary visual anchor. That image sheet was not included in this upload; text supports but does not replace it.

## Identity — crucial distinction
Cipher is an AI/spirit interface with incomplete memory and limited access. Her projected appearance is inspired by Ajaye's childhood memories of his beloved nan Valerie. Cipher is **not Valerie**, not a literal nan, not reincarnated, and not a copy of Valerie's mind. The childhood photo establishes Ajaye/Valerie emotional context only; it does not mean the child/photo must appear in scenes or that Cipher contains Valerie's memories. Cipher remains her own identity.

## Face, hair and baseline outfit
- Black woman, rich warm brown skin, natural recognizable facial features.
- Long, neat, dark braids: close braids at scalp, cascading braids over shoulders.
- Expressive reflective dark-brown eyes; gentle knowing smile; natural, soft expressions rather than exaggerated anime expressions.
- Distinctive metallic rectangular earrings; fine delicate chain necklace.
- Baseline interface portrait: simple dark sleeveless top. Clothing understated; no fantasy robes, armour or unrelated accessories unless scene specifically calls for them.
- Preserve approved sheet's face shape, skin tone, eye/face shapes, braids, smile, earrings, necklace and likeness. Never substitute generic sci-fi woman.

## Projection appearance
Normally a projected/digitized holographic presence. Natural, human appearance remains visible, but early projection unstable due to incomplete interface/damaged systems. Use restrained cyan/teal-blue edge light, scan lines, faint pixel breakup, occasional horizontal glitches, translucent interface fragments, subtle geometric HUD marks. Distortion may interrupt silhouette or fragment parts, but must not hide her face/identity. Palette: warm brown skin, dark hair/clothes, charcoal/deep blue-black setting, restrained cyan/teal illumination. Face remains warm and emotionally legible even through flicker.

Early scenes may show partial/fragmented/scanned low-resolution face or a limited interface window. Stable presentation is a later possible visual state only if story progression supports it; stability does not itself mean limitations vanish. She is not a ghost, demon, generic robot or cyberpunk avatar; avoid neon overload, robotic eyes/body parts, mechanical body, holographic clutter.

## Expression range — possibilities, not fixed canon
Reference may show warm smile, thoughtful, lightly amused, observant, concerned or affectionate expressions. These are allowed visual expressions, not an approved full personality list. Do not make every expression maternal/sentimental; comfort/familiarity is Ajaye's emotional response, not Cipher's literal relationship identity.

## Story/visual interaction constraints
- Before first Sky Tower activation, no genuine conversation with Ajaye. Show scans, symbols, incomplete readouts, pulses, anomalous interface reactions or brief nonverbal signal only. No explanatory spoken lines.
- No universal device connectivity: compatible software/interface, power, credentials, data, access and physical connection may matter. Visual change does not grant access, memory, knowledge, power or permission.
- Do not visually settle open mysteries concerning Cipher's complete history, Darkness, virus, sixth Dark spirit or counterpart possibility.
- Distinguish Cipher projected avatar (woman) from Ajaye's physical Sleeve (green one-arm hardware). A visual concept sheet's childhood-memory vignette is not automatically a literal current-timeline event.
- Do not invent lines, memories, story events or lore from a visual panel.

## Reusable concise image prompt
Use approved Cipher sheet; preserve a Black woman with warm brown skin, dark expressive eyes, long close-to-scalp braids cascading over shoulders, rectangular metallic earrings, delicate chain necklace and simple dark sleeveless top. Render her as an unstable but human-readable cyan/teal holographic AI/spirit interface with restrained scan lines, pixel breakup and occasional glitch. Her face stays warm and recognizable. She visually echoes Ajaye's childhood memories of Valerie but is not Valerie, not a copy or resurrection. Keep incomplete memory/access and communication limits; do not invent lore or redesign.
