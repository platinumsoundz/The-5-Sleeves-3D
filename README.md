# The 5 Sleeves — Training Ground

Serve over HTTP and open localhost:8000. Requires WebGL and Three.js CDN. This is a procedural settlement training scene with low-poly character whiteboxes; no external art assets are required.

Keyboard/mouse (standard shooter layout): WASD/arrows move, left-click staff attack (click, not drag), right-click hold to aim/lock, Space jump/get up, Shift sprint, Ctrl crouch, Q (or K) guard — tap to block, direction to dodge, time it just before impact to parry — F interact, Enter confirm; drag to orbit and pitch the camera. Standard gamepad: left stick move, right stick look, LT aim/lock, RT staff attack, A jump/get up, B guard (direction dodges, timed parry), X interact/confirm, L3 sprint, R3 crouch. Touch uses a mobile-shooter layout: analog stick bottom-left, drag the right side to look, hold AIM to zoom/lock and a FIRE button appears over the left thumb; the other buttons sit bottom-right. Tutorial guides movement, staff attack, aim/lock, and guard/dodge/parry and can be skipped. Sensei is passive until spar, then telegraphs with a HUD "!" before striking. Both fighters can be knocked down and recover; a downed player must confirm to get up. Knockdowns score and the first to 3 takes the spar; three blocked hits in a row break the guard. Restart practice appears after tutorial. MENU (top-right) or Esc pauses and opens look sensitivity, invert-Y and graphics-quality settings, stored locally. The Cipher Sleeve is not acquired in this opening scene, so its HUD meter is hidden and Ajaye has only wrapped forearms. XP/currency and Cipher powers are deferred. The owner browser-tested the pre-visual build at a reported 120 FPS; the 2026-10-22 settlement/character visual pass still needs its own browser check. Run `npm test` for the 53 headless checks. See docs, including AI provenance in `docs/memory/04-ai-collaboration.md` and the build log in `docs/ai-build-log.md`.
